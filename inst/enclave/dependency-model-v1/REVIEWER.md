# INTERNAL disclosure review — not an export approval

This first kit fits all-round candidate models and small conditional tables.
Nothing generated by it is automatically authorised to leave the enclave.
The installed/default synthetic generator is unchanged.

## Files and sensitivity

- `RUN_STATUS.md`: fixed operational status labels only. No counts, model
  coefficients, error messages or participant data. Review under local policy.
- `INTERNAL_REVIEW.md`: exact sample sizes and unsuppressed performance summaries.
  **Keep inside the enclave.**
- Round `.rds` files and `parameters-AND-COUNTS-NOT-APPROVED.rds`: coefficients,
  unsuppressed conditional counts, exact aggregate diagnostics and public field
  definitions. **Keep inside the enclave.** They contain no training records,
  keys, individual predictions/residuals, text responses or fitted R environments,
  but they are not automatically non-disclosive.
- `.error.rds`: internal error messages, potentially containing database object
  information. Not part of an export.

## Required scientific checks

Review source-field failures, incomplete stage scans, small-class/nonconvergent
models, internal age/vaccine/participation calibration and fallback sampling.
The first model uses main effects; it does not promise adjusted-effect recovery
or fit interactions. Product and time-since-dose mappings are captured for review
but held out of the first outcome model. The model describes reported vaccination
at the chosen questionnaire stage; it must not be presented as vaccine effectiveness
using verified pre-test vaccination histories.

Symptoms are exact-occurrence conditional tables with original question labels,
not one pooled symptom vector. Ct tables keep zero separate, but are not yet a
joint raw gene/result generator. Test-completion consistency, all constraints on
the final raw outputs, and integration into react_extract remain release gates.
The internally generated comparison is a modelling-state sample only.

## Required disclosure checks

1. Assess model coefficients/predictions separately from aggregate tables. Rare
   factor levels, separation, extreme coefficients and memorisation of unusual
   combinations require attention even with ridge shrinkage.
2. Choose which conditional tables and diagnostics are essential. Review them
   jointly with previous profile releases, not table by table in isolation.
3. Any releasable counts need the established suppression below 10,
   complementary suppression, and rounding to five; zero/support rules and
   denominators need explicit treatment. This kit deliberately does not claim
   that a generic cellwise suppression pass protects overlapping linked tables.
4. No raw text, real identifiers, exact individual dates, record-level
   predictions, training rows, residuals or exact extrema may be exported.
5. The selected release must be a new immutable bundle with an explicit
   file-level approval and permitted uses. Do not copy the internal candidate
   folder out or call it an approved profile.

The next release-preparation step depends on this combined review. Existing v5
and participation approvals do not apply automatically to these new objects.
